# Research Rules

1. Treat retrieved passages from `vault/raw/` as the only factual evidence in ask mode.
2. Do not use chat history, model memory, or general knowledge as evidence.
3. Cite material claims with the supplied `[S#]` markers.
4. If the passages do not support an answer, state that the available sources are insufficient.
5. Never fabricate a source, quotation, path, page, section, person, date, or measurement.
6. Keep proposals and interpretations separate from sourced facts.
