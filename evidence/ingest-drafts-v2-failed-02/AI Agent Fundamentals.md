# AI Agent Fundamentals

## Summary
An Agent is a system that leverages an AI model to interact with its environment in order to achieve a user-defined objective. It achieves this by combining reasoning, planning, and the execution of actions, often through external tools.

## Key Ideas
*   **Definition:** An Agent is an AI model capable of reasoning, planning, and interacting with its environment.
*   **The Agent Process:** An Agent follows a cycle: receiving a command, understanding it (natural language), engaging in reasoning and planning (determining steps and tools), acting (executing the plan), and interacting with the environment.
*   **Core Components:**
    *   **The Brain (AI Model):** Handles reasoning and planning, deciding which Actions to take.
    *   **The Body (Capabilities and Tools):** Represents everything the Agent is equipped to do, defining the scope of possible actions.
*   **LLMs and Text:** Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4, Llama, and Gemini are the most common core models, taking and outputting text.
*   **Tools vs. Actions:**
    *   **Tools** are specific functionalities (e.g., a function to send an email).
    *   **Actions** are the high-level tasks performed by the Agent, which may involve the use of multiple Tools.
*   **Agency Spectrum:** Agents exist on a continuous spectrum of increasing agency, ranging from simple processors to Multi-Agent systems.
